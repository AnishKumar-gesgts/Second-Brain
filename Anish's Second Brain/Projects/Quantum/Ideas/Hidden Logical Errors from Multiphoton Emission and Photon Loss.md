# Hidden Logical Errors from Multiphoton Emission and Photon Loss

[[Ideas]]

This is **Idea 1**, to be studied after Ideas 2 and 3 as a second compiler-validation case. The mechanism is

$$
\text{two photons emitted}
\rightarrow
\text{one photon lost}
\rightarrow
\text{one photon detected}.
$$

An apparently valid detector record can therefore conceal a faulty physical event. The optical model must distinguish hidden Fock-state history from the detector/herald record, while the compiled QEC model gives the decoder only the latter.

Start with one explicit fusion/interference primitive with \(g^{(2)}\), loss, visibility, and PNR-efficiency parameters. Verify ideal-source and independent-loss controls, probability conservation, Fock-cutoff convergence, and decoder-visible event classification before scale-up. Early results remain limited to the stated primitive and compiled circuit-level QEC model—not hardware performance or a general threshold.
